Thesis AI User Analytics

This analysis explores Thesis AI user growth, subscription activity, citation preferences, fields of study, and AI usage behaviour.

Key Findings
671 total users were recorded.
New user sign-ups peaked in May 2026.
Only 8 users are on a paid plan, indicating a low conversion rate.
Most users prefer Human-in-the-loop AI interaction.
APA 7 is the most commonly used citation style.
Computer Science and Economics are among the most common fields of study.

The analysis was performed using Python, Pandas, Matplotlib, and Seaborn
